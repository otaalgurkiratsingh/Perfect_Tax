# Staff guide — one page

**Signing in:** go to `/staff`, enter your email and password, then the 6-digit code from your authenticator
app. You're signed out after 30 minutes of no activity, and always after 12 hours. Forgot your password? Use
"Forgot your password?" on the sign-in page.

## New clients (the intake form)

- **Where clients start:** send them the link (`/start`) by text, email or WhatsApp, or let them scan the
  **Office QR** poster (Dashboard → *Office QR* → print). For an office tablet, open `/start?kiosk=1`: the tablet
  forgets their answers after they finish.
- **Inbox** (Dashboard home): a row for every form, newest first. The tabs across the top are the pipeline:
  **New → In Review → Docs Missing → Ready → Client Created**. *Unfinished* = people who started but didn't
  send (the system reminds them twice, then stops). Use the search box for a name, email, business or reference
  number. The "Heard about us" column shows where clients come from.
- **Open a row** to see every answer and document. The SIN shows as `••• ••• 123`. *Reveal (logged)* shows it
  for 30 seconds and records who looked. *View* opens a document in the browser and *Download* saves it. Both are
  recorded.
- **Change the status** with the *Pipeline status* box at the top right.
- **Something missing?** Click *Request missing docs*, tick the items and press *Send*. The client gets a private
  link that only accepts those items. Reminders go weekly, three at most. When everything arrives, the status
  changes to *In Review* on its own and a green "Missing documents received" note appears.
- **Download all (ZIP)** gives one file with every document plus a summary of the answers.
- **Export CSV** (top of the inbox) gives a spreadsheet of the current view. It never contains SINs.
- **Create client** turns a finished intake into an ongoing client for quarterly collection.

## Every quarter (bank statements, pay stubs, cheques…)

1. Dashboard → **Quarterly** → pick the year and quarter → tick **Send requests to all clients now** → *Open quarter*.
   Every active client gets their own checklist and a private upload link by text and email.
2. The **board** shows every client as **Not Sent / Requested / Partial / Complete / Processed**, with how many
   checklist items are in. Reminders go out automatically on days 7, 14, 21 and 28, but **only** to clients who
   haven't finished. When a client completes their checklist, their reminders stop on the spot.
3. *Nudge* sends one extra reminder now. *Nudge all stragglers* does it for everyone still waiting.
4. Click a client's name to see their files. If something isn't right (e.g. a month is missing), click
   *Mark as still missing* and they'll be reminded about it.
5. When you've finished their work, click **Mark processed**.
6. **Bank statement transactions (Beta):** on a client's quarter page, click *Read all statements*, then
   *Download CSV*. Check the colour: **green/high** means the totals add up. **Amber or red** means check the
   flagged rows against the statement. **Manual review** means it was a scanned photo, so type it as usual. This
   saves typing. It doesn't replace your judgment.

## Clients' own page

On a client's record, *Invite to client page* emails them a sign-in link (no password). There they can upload
this quarter's documents and see past quarters.

## Admins only

- **Audit log**: who viewed, downloaded, exported or changed what, and when. It can't be edited.
- **Settings**: the go-live checklist, the retention period (old intakes are *flagged* in the "Deletion review"
  tab, never deleted automatically), and adding or turning off staff accounts.
- **Delete** (on an intake): permanently removes it and its files after a retention review. Type the reference
  number to confirm.

**Privacy rules:** never email or text a SIN or documents. Use the request-docs link instead. Don't download
files to shared computers. If a client asks to stop messages, they can reply STOP, or tell an admin.
